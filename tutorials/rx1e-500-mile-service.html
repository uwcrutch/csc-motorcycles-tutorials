RX1E 500 Mile Service

CSC Motorcycles — Archived Maintenance Tutorial

Testing Page.html

 

In this video, you will learn how to perform the first 500-mile service of routine maintenance on your CSC Motorcycles RX1E electric motorcycle. The RX1E is a new mid-power electric motorcycle that can hit highway speeds and has a range of 112 miles on a single charge. The video will show you how to inspect and adjust the throttle cable, the brake levers, the brake pads, the tire pressure, the chain tension, and the steering head bearings. The video will guide you step by step through the maintenance process and provide you with helpful tips and tricks along the way. By following this video, you will be able to keep your RX1E in optimal condition and enjoy a smooth and comfortable ride. You can find a service checklist in the Owner's Manual for the RX1E.
