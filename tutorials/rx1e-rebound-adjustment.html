In this video, we show you how to adjust the rebound on the front and rear suspension on the CSC RX1E electric motorcycle. The RX1E is the latest and most powerful electric motorcycle from CSC Motorcycles, the company that brought you the City Slicker, the Wiz, and the Monterey. The RX1E has a 8kw water-cooled motor that can reach up to 80 mph, a range of 100 miles per charge, and a low price for all of the features of an electric motorcycle. It also has a reverse gear, ABS brakes, LED lights, and a low seat height of 30.9 inches. The RX1E is the best value electric motorcycle for real world use.

Adjusting the rebound on both the front forks and the rear shock is easy with a small screwdriver. You just need to locate the rebound adjuster screw on each suspension component and turn it clockwise or counterclockwise depending on your preference. Rebound adjustment affects how quickly the suspension returns to its original position after being compressed. A faster rebound can make the ride more responsive and agile, while a slower rebound can make it more stable and comfortable.
