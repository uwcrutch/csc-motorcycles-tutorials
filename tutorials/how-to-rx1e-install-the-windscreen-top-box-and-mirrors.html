How to Install Windscreen and Top Box on RX1E

Congratulations on your new RX1e! This electric motorcycle is a great way to get around town or on the highway. To make your RX1e even more enjoyable, you can install a windscreen and top box. These accessories will help to protect you from the wind and rain, and give you a place to store your belongings.

What you will need:

  • Windscreen
  • Top box
  • Hardware kit
  • 5mm Allen wrench
  • 8mm wrench
  • 14mm wrench (for the mirrors)

Instructions:

  1. Install the top box.
    1. Remove the felt mat from the bottom of the top box.
    2. Place the rubber mat in the bottom of the top box.
    3. Line up the holes in the bottom of the top box with the threaded holes in the pan.
    4. Insert each of the four screws and hat washers through the holes and screw them down.
    5. Replace the felt mat in the bottom of the top box.
  2. Install the windscreen.
    1. Set up your screws and plastic washers.
    2. Use the 8mm wrench for your acorn nuts and your 5mm for all your screws.
    3. Insert a hat washer through each hole in the windscreen from the passenger side.
    4. Screw the screws through the hat washers and into the motorcycle.
    5. Tighten all of the screws.
  3. Install the mirrors.
    1. Attach the left mirror to the motorcycle.
    2. Attach the right mirror to the motorcycle.
    3. Tighten the nuts on both mirrors.

Tips:

  • Make sure that all of the screws are tightened securely.
  • Do not overtighten the screws, as this could damage the motorcycle.
  • If you are not comfortable installing the windscreen and top box yourself, you can take your RX1E to a qualified mechanic.
