Wiz front headlight removal

Tools needed

Phillips head screwdriver

5mm hex head driver







Step 1: place bike on center stand

 

Step 2: remove 3 phillips head screws on the left side of the fairing

 

Step 3: Remove 2 Phillips head screw behind the front wheel

 

 

 

Step 4: remove 2 phillips head screws holding front fairing to the side panels

 

 

 

 

Step 5: remove 3 phillps head screws on right side of inner fairing

 

Step 6: remove 2 phillips head screws on holding front fairing to side panels

Step 7: remove 5mm screw from front of fairing

 

 

 

 

Step 8: remove 2 phillips head screws holding front fairing to inner fairing below the front cowl

 

 

 

 

Step 9: once all the phillips head screws and the 5mm bolt pry front fairing out starting from the side covers to the front

Step 10: lean fairing forward to access head light assembly and wiring harness, disconnect plug from headlight

 

Step 11 remove 5 phillips head screws holding headlight assembly to front fairing

Step 12 remove headlight from assembly

Step 13: remove dust cover from headlight

 

 

 

 

Step 14: to replace lightbulb remove blue screw

 

 

 

 

Step 15: bulb can now be removed and replaced

Step 16: once new bulb is installed place bracket in this position, tighten the screw and slide the other end of the bracket underneath the slot on the opposite side

 

 

Step 17: install the dust cover and screw the headlight assembly back to the front fairing

 

 

 

 

Step 18: reconnect headlight to wiring harness

Final step: reattach front fairing from the top-down finishing on the side covers, follow instructions in reverse to secure fairing to inner fairing
