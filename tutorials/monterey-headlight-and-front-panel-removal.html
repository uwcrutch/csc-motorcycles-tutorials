Removing the Front Panel & Headlight on Your CSC Monterey Scooter

This tutorial guides Monterey owners through safely removing the front panel and headlight for maintenance or repairs.

What You'll Need:

  • #2 Phillips Screwdriver
  • Needle Nose Pliers
  • Flat Screwdriver

Removing the Headlight:

  1. Locate the 2 fasteners (screws with washers) securing the headlight.
  2. Use the #2 Phillips screwdriver to remove these fasteners.
  3. Twist the headlight counter-clockwise to release it from its locking tabs (demonstrated in the video).
  4. Disconnect the electrical connector by pressing the quick-release tab.
  5. The headlight is now free.

Removing the Front Panel (Cowling):

  1. Remove the 4 fasteners (two on each side) holding the front panel.
  2. Carefully release the 2 plastic auto-body clips (removal tips shown in video).
  3. Gently but firmly release the panel from its multiple plastic locking tabs (locations highlighted in video).
  4. Once loose, disconnect the wiring for the turn signals and marker lights.
  5. The front panel is now completely removed.

Why Remove the Front Panel?

This access allows you to work on or replace components like:

  • The dash/speedometer
  • Speedometer cable
  • Brake cables
  • Brake light switches
  • Troubleshoot electrical issues

About Your CSC Monterey Electric Scooter:

The Monterey blends iconic retro style with modern electric convenience. Key features include:

  • Effortless Riding: No clutch, no gears, no pedaling - just twist and go!
  • Electric Power: 2400W motor, 30 mph top speed, ~20-30 mile range.
  • Removable Battery: 60V lithium battery (22 lbs) charges anywhere (110V-220V).
  • Practical & Comfortable: 30" seat height, lockable storage, USB port, hydraulic suspension, drum brakes.
