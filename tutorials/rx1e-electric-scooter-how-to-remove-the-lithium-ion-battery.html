RX1E Electric Scooter: How to Remove the Lithium Ion Battery

CSC Motorcycles — Archived Maintenance Tutorial

Testing Page.html

 

 

In this video, we will show you how to remove and replace the lithium-ion battery from the CSC RX1E electric motorcycle. The RX1E is a low-cost electric motorcycle that offers a smooth and eco-friendly ride. With a powerful motor, three ride modes, and a long range, the RX1E is ideal for urban commuters and adventurers. The RX1E uses a lithium iron phosphate battery that is lighter, safer, and more durable than conventional lead-acid batteries. The battery has a capacity of 72V and 40Ah, and can be fully charged in about four hours using a standard 110V outlet.

To remove and replace the battery, you will need the following tools:

  • 4mm Allen
  • 5mm Allen
  • 8mm socket
  • 10mm socket
  • 12mm socket
  • #2 Phillips screwdriver
  • Socket extension
  • Swivel head ratchet
  • Marker

You will also need a safe place to store the old battery and a new battery to install.
