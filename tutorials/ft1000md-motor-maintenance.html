How to Grease the Bafang M620 Mid-Drive Motor on Your FT1000MD E-Bike

Maintaining your FT1000MD e-bike’s Bafang M620 mid-drive motor is crucial for ensuring its longevity and optimal performance. Regular greasing can significantly reduce noise, improve efficiency, and extend the life of your motor. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you through the process.

Why Grease Your Motor?

CSC Motorcycles recommends regreasing your motor after the first 300 miles and at least once a year after that. If the motor becomes noisier or warmer than normal, we recommend performing this maintenance sooner. This is because the motors are manufactured with a light application of grease that tends to break down more quickly than the greases commonly available in the United States. Regular maintenance is especially important if you frequently ride in hilly terrain, at high speeds, or carry heavy loads.

Signs That Your Motor Needs Greasing

  • Increased noise from the motor
  • Reduced performance
  • Overheating

If you notice any of these signs, it’s time to grease your motor.

Tools and Materials Needed

  • High-quality grease suitable for e-bike motors
  • Screwdrivers
  • Cleaning cloths
  • Gloves

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Preparation: Ensure your e-bike is turned off and securely positioned. Remove the battery for safety.
  2. Access the Motor: Depending on your e-bike model, you may need to remove the crank arms and motor cover. Refer to your e-bike’s manual for specific instructions.
  3. Clean the Motor: Before applying new grease, clean the motor gears to remove any old grease and debris. Use a clean cloth to wipe down the gears.
  4. Apply Grease: Apply a generous amount of high-quality grease to the motor gears. Make sure the grease is evenly distributed.
  5. Reassemble the Motor: Once the gears are greased, reassemble the motor cover and crank arms.
  6. Test the Motor: Turn on your e-bike and take it for a short ride to ensure everything is functioning smoothly.

Additional Tips

  • Frequency: Grease your motor every 300 miles or sooner if you ride in harsh conditions.
  • Quality: Use high-quality grease to ensure the best performance and longevity.
  • Professional Help: If you’re unsure about any step, consider seeking professional assistance.

Regular maintenance of your Bafang M620 mid-drive motor will keep your FT1000MD e-bike running smoothly and efficiently.
