In this video, we show you how to connect the charger and what the blink codes mean for the RX1E charger. The RX1E is our electric motorcycle that offers a smooth and silent ride with zero emissions. It’s perfect for city commuting or weekend adventures.

The charger is separate from the motorcycle and can be plugged into any standard 110-volt outlet in the US. It has a power rating of 3300 watts and a current rating of 16 amps. It weighs about 15 lbs and has a sturdy design that makes it durable and reliable.

The charger has one LED light that blinks in different patterns to show the charging status and any possible errors. We explain what each blink code means and how to troubleshoot any issues.

Whether you are an existing, future, or potential owner of the RX1E, this video will help you understand how to charge your electric motorcycle safely and efficiently. The charger is used to charge the 96-volt, 64Ah battery on the RX1E, which gives it a range of up to 100 miles per charge.

Here are some of the benefits of owning an electric motorcycle:

  • Zero emissions: Electric motorcycles produce no harmful emissions, making them a great choice for the environment.
  • Low maintenance costs: Electric motorcycles have fewer moving parts than gasoline-powered motorcycles, which means they require less maintenance.
  • Quiet operation: Electric motorcycles are much quieter than gasoline-powered motorcycles, making them a great choice for city riding.
