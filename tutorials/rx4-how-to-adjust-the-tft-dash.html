TRANSCRIPT

Description of the dash

TFT stand for Thin Film Transistor Liquid Crystal Display

Automatically changes color depending on light conditions for maximum visibility.

2 display formats to choose from

The TFT dash has a mileage computer which averages your speed and fuel consumption

The tire pressure and temperature monitor will let you know when you know if you are in the ideal pressure and temperature range for traction and safety and alert you of too low or too high tire pressures.

Coolant temperature is also indicated in Celsius. To reference a range, 180 Fahrenheit is 82 Celsius and 220 Fahrenheit is 104 Celsius

Bluetooth connectivity for Caller ID

There are simply only two buttons to adjust the dash the left “MODE” button and the right “SELECT” button.

Setting time-

Turn on ignition and wait for dash to complete start-up cycle

Hold down BOTH “Mode” and “Select” buttons at the same time for at least 3 seconds to put the clock into setting mode, as indicated by the flashing time

With hours (00:00) flashing tap the LEFT “Mode” button to set desired hour

Tap the RIGHT “select” button to switch over to minutes

With the minutes (00:00) flashing tap the LEFT “Mode” button to set minutes

If no adjustment is made within 5 seconds, clock will leave the setting mode and the clock numbers will become static

You have the option between 12 and 24hr time format. With clock is still flashing, holding down the “Mode” button will set the clock to 12hr time and holding down the “Select” button will set the clock to 24hr time

Changing KPH to MPH

With the ignition off

Hold down the RIGHT “Select” button while turning on ignition.

Keep the RIGHT “Select” button depressed until speedometer reads the desired MPH or KPH. It will take about 10 seconds for the change to happen.

Changing sceen format-

There are (2) two formats: “Analog tachometer” and “Linear tachometer”

To switch between the two, turn on ignition

There are two MPG settings, current MPG and average MPG. Tap the LEFT “Mode” button to scroll fuel and milage computer to current MPG

Hold down the RIGHT “Select” button until the screen resets, then release the button.

The new format will appear.

The Light sensor on will change the color of the TFT dash automatically depending on light conditions for maximum visibility

Clearing mileage calculator

Turn on ignition and wait for dash to complete the start-up cycle

Select which value you want to reset by tapping “Mode” (left) button. Values will cycle between

MAX SPEED, AVERAGE SPEED, CURRENT MPG, and AVERAGE MPG

Holding down “Select” (right) button will zero the value

In the “Linear Tachometer” dash format, the mileage calculator can turn off by tapping the “Mode” (left) button and scrolling to the “ON” setting in the highlighted box, this will change the setting to “OFF”. Holding down “Select” (right) button will turn off the calculator from the screen for a cleaner display. Holding down the “Select” (right) button will bring back mileage calculator.

Caller I.D.

Scan for Bluetooth devices and select “CYCLONE”. Enter password 0000. When your phone is connected, the Bluetooth icon will turn blue. Incoming phone numbers will appear on dash

TPMS sensors

Front and rear tire pressure and temperature sensors have been installed and synced at the factory. Values are metric only (bar, Celsius)

Tire pressure is indicated in BAR. (1) BAR if 14.7 PSI.

The tire pressure will indicate in RED if not within the minimum and maximum pressure range, the front tire minimum is 29psi or 2.0 bar and the maximum of 43psi or 3 bar. The rear tire minimum is 33 psi or 2.27 bar and a maximum of 49psi 3.37bar.

Tire temperature is indicated in Celsius. 165F or 74 Celsius is considered the ideal tire temperature for traction.

Resetting maintenance reminder

A yellow wrench will appear on the dash to remind you that service is due. It will first appear at 1,500 kilometers (932 miles) then every 3,000 kilometers (1,864 miles) after that.

To clear the reminder, tap the “Mode” button to cycle the milage computer to current MPG (it is the only metric that does not have a prefix of “AVG” or “MAX” before it)

When you are in the current MPG setting, hold down “select” until the yellow wrench disappears.
