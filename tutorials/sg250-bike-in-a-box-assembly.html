Looking to assemble your new CSC SG250 "Bike in a Box"? You've come to the right place! CSC Motorcycles has created a comprehensive, step-by-step video guide to make the process of assembling your unassembled motorcycle smooth and easy. This series of 10 videos covers every detail, from the moment your bike arrives in its crate to the final pre-driving inspections.

The playlist begins with a video that shows you the best way to safely uncrate your SG250 and get started. Subsequent videos provide detailed instructions for key assembly steps, including:

  • Installing the Handlebars: Learn how to correctly install your handlebars using a 6mm Allen, wire cutters, a #2 Phillips screwdriver, and more.

  • Mounting the Front Axle and Wheel: Follow along as the guide walks you through this critical step with a list of essential tools like 12mm, 15mm, and 17mm sockets or wrenches.

  • Installing the Rear Brake Lever, Footpegs, and Exhaust: Get clear instructions on these important components, with a breakdown of the specific tools you'll need.

  • Attaching the Side Stand, Front Fender, and Mirrors: Finish up the main assembly with videos dedicated to each of these parts.

  • Wiring the Taillight and Turn Signals: The guide covers the electrical components, ensuring you have your lights and signals ready to go.

  • Performing Pre-Driving Inspections: The final video is crucial, focusing on all the necessary safety checks and inspections to ensure your motorcycle is ready for the road.

This playlist is the definitive resource for anyone who has purchased a CSC "Bike in a Box" and wants a clear, visual guide to assembly.
